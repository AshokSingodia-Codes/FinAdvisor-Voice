# SECTION 1: INDIAN INCOME TAX STATUTORY FRAMEWORK (UP TO SEPTEMBER 2026)

## 1.1 Current Assessment Years & Filing Timelines (September 2026 Context)
* **Current Fiscal Year (Financial Year)**: **FY 2026-27** (April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027).
* **Current Assessment Year (AY)**: **AY 2027-28** for income earned in FY 2026-27.
* **Assessment Year for Recent Filings**: **AY 2026-27** for income earned in FY 2025-26.
* **Default Statutory Tax Regime**: **Section 115BAC (New Tax Regime)** is the universal default regime for Individuals, HUFs, AOPs, and BOIs.

### Opt-Out / Opt-In Protocol (Form 10-IEA):
* **Salaried Individuals (No Business Income)**: Can toggle between the New and Old Tax Regimes annually when filing their ITR-1 or ITR-2 under Section 139(1).
* **Business / Professional Taxpayers (ITR-3 / ITR-4)**: Must file **Form 10-IEA** on or before the due date under Section 139(1) to opt for the Old Regime. Re-entry into the New Regime is allowed only once in a lifetime while continuing business operations.

## 1.2 Section 115BAC Progressive Slab Rates (Applicable FY 2024-25, FY 2025-26 & FY 2026-27)

| Net Annual Taxable Income (₹) | Statutory Tax Rate | Marginal Tax Calculation Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **Up to ₹3,00,000** | **Nil (0%)** | ₹0 |
| **₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000** | **5%** | $5\% \times (\text{Taxable Income} - ₹3,00,000)$ |
| **₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000** | **10%** | $₹20,000 + 10\% \times (\text{Taxable Income} - ₹7,00,000)$ |
| **₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000** | **15%** | $₹50,000 + 15\% \times (\text{Taxable Income} - ₹10,00,000)$ |
| **₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000** | **20%** | $₹80,000 + 20\% \times (\text{Taxable Income} - ₹12,00,000)$ |
| **Above ₹15,00,000** | **30%** | $₹1,40,000 + 30\% \times (\text{Taxable Income} - ₹15,00,000)$ |

### Statutory Allowances & Rebate Benchmarks Under Section 115BAC:
1. **Standard Deduction (Section 16(ia))**: **₹75,000** for salaried employees and pensioners. (Family pensioners receive ₹25,000).
2. **Section 87A Tax Rebate**:
   * Resident individuals with Net Taxable Income $\le ₹7,00,000$ receive a full tax rebate of up to **₹25,000**, reducing tax liability to **₹0**.
   * **Effective Zero-Tax Threshold**: A salaried individual with Gross Salary up to **₹7,75,000** pays **₹0 Income Tax** ($₹7,75,000 - ₹75,000\text{ standard deduction} = ₹7,00,000\text{ taxable income} \rightarrow \text{rebate wipes out ₹25,000 tax}$).
3. **Employer NPS Contribution (Section 80CCD(2))**:
   * Deductible up to **14% of Salary** (Basic + DA) for both Government and Private Sector employees.
4. **Marginal Relief Mechanism**:
   * Ensures that if taxable income marginally exceeds ₹7,00,000, the income tax payable cannot exceed the incremental income earned above ₹7,00,000.

## 1.3 Old Tax Regime & Deductions Framework (Applicable by Opt-Out)

### Old Regime Progressive Slabs:
| Taxable Income Slab | Individual (<60 Years) | Senior Citizen (60–79 Years) | Super Senior Citizen (≥80 Years) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Up to ₹2,50,000** | Nil | Nil | Nil |
| **₹2,50,001 to ₹3,00,000** | 5% | Nil | Nil |
| **₹3,00,001 to ₹5,00,000** | 5% | 5% | Nil |
| **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000** | 20% | 20% | 20% |
| **Above ₹10,00,000** | 30% | 30% | 30% |

### Key Chapter VI-A & Salary Allowances (Old Regime Only):
* **Standard Deduction**: ₹50,000 (Salaried).
* **Section 80C (Ceiling ₹1,50,000)**: ELSS Mutual Funds (3-year lock-in), PPF, EPF, VPF, NSC, SSY, 5-year Tax-Saving FDs, Home loan principal repayment, Life insurance premium, Children's tuition fees.
* **Section 80CCD(1B)**: Dedicated **₹50,000** deduction for voluntary employee contribution to Tier-1 NPS over and above Section 80C.
* **Section 80D (Health Insurance)**: Up to **₹25,000** for self/family (₹50,000 if senior citizen) + additional **₹25,000** for parents (₹50,000 if senior citizen parents). Maximum combined ceiling = **₹1,00,000**.
* **Section 24(b) (Home Loan Interest)**: Up to **₹2,00,000** for self-occupied residential property.
* **Section 10(13A) (HRA Exemption)**: Minimum of (1) Actual HRA received, (2) Rent paid $- 10\%$ of Basic+DA, or (3) $50\%$ Basic+DA (Metro) / $40\%$ (Non-Metro).
* **Section 80E**: $100\%$ deduction on higher education loan interest for up to 8 years without upper limit.
* **Section 80TTA / 80TTB**: Savings account interest up to ₹10,000 (80TTA); All deposit interest up to ₹50,000 for Senior Citizens (80TTB).

## 1.4 Capital Gains Tax Framework (Post-Budget 2024 & Active in 2026)

| Capital Asset Category | Short-Term Holding Period | STCG Tax Rate | Long-Term Holding Period | LTCG Tax Rate | Statutory Exemptions & Indexation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Listed Equity Shares & Equity Mutual Funds** | $\le 12\text{ Months}$ | **20.0%** (Section 111A) | $> 12\text{ Months}$ | **12.5%** (Section 112A) | **₹1,25,000 annual LTCG exemption** per financial year. STT paid. No indexation. |
| **Unlisted Equity Shares** | $\le 24\text{ Months}$ | Applicable Slab Rate | $> 24\text{ Months}$ | **12.5%** (Section 112) | Indexation benefit abolished. |
| **Immovable Real Estate (Land/House)** | $\le 24\text{ Months}$ | Applicable Slab Rate | $> 24\text{ Months}$ | **12.5%** (Without indexation) | Pre-July 23, 2024 acquisitions have option to pay $20\%$ with indexation if lower. |
| **Debt Mutual Funds & Specified Funds ($\le 35\%$ Equity)** | All holding periods | Applicable Slab Rate | All holding periods | Applicable Slab Rate | Deemed short-term capital assets under Section 50AA. |
| **Gold / Precious Metals / Gold ETFs / Physical Gold** | $\le 24\text{ Months}$ | Applicable Slab Rate | $> 24\text{ Months}$ | **12.5%** (Section 112) | Indexation benefit abolished. SGB held to 8-year maturity is $100\%$ tax-exempt. |
| **Share Buyback Receipts (Post-Oct 2024 / 2026)** | N/A | Taxed as Deemed Dividend at slab rate | N/A | Deemed Dividend at slab rate | Cost of original shares allowed as capital loss to be set off. |

### Capital Gains Reinvestment Reliefs:
* **Section 54**: LTCG exemption from residential house sale if reinvested in another residential house in India within 1 yr before / 2 yrs after / 3 yrs construction (capped at ₹10 Crores).
* **Section 54EC**: Up to **₹50,00,000** LTCG exemption on real estate sale if invested in REC/NHAI/PFC/IRFC bonds within 6 months (5-year lock-in).
* **Section 54F**: LTCG exemption on non-residential assets if net consideration is reinvested in a residential house.
* **Loss Set-off & Carry Forward**: STCL can offset STCG and LTCG. LTCL can **only** offset LTCG. Unabsorbed losses carry forward for up to **8 assessment years**.

## 1.5 Advance Tax, TDS & Derivative STT Slabs (2026 Standards)

### Advance Tax Installment Schedule (Section 208/211):
Applicable if net tax liability exceeds ₹10,000 in the fiscal year:
1. **By June 15**: 15% of total estimated tax.
2. **By September 15**: 45% of total estimated tax.
3. **By December 15**: 75% of total estimated tax.
4. **By March 15**: 100% of total estimated tax.
*Penalties*: Section 234A (delay in filing), Section 234B (shortfall in payment $<90\%$), Section 234C (installment deferment) at $1\%/\text{month}$ simple interest.

### Securities Transaction Tax (STT) on Derivatives (Active in 2026):
* **Futures**: $0.02\%$ on turnover.
* **Options**: $0.1\%$ on option premium turnover.

### Key TDS Slabs:
* **Section 192**: Salary TDS at slab rates.
* **Section 194A**: Bank interest TDS at $10\%$ if exceeding ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
* **Section 194K**: Mutual fund dividend income TDS at $10\%$ if exceeding ₹5,000.
* **Section 194S**: Virtual Digital Assets (Crypto/VDA) transfers at $1\%$ TDS.

# SECTION 2: MUTUAL FUNDS, PORTFOLIO MATHEMATICS & WEALTH ARCHITECTURE

## 2.1 Complete SEBI Mutual Fund Categorization
* **Large Cap Fund**: $\ge 80\%$ in top 100 stocks. Lowest equity drawdown, high liquidity.
* **Mid Cap Fund**: $\ge 65\%$ in 101st to 250th stocks. Higher earnings growth, cyclical volatility.
* **Small Cap Fund**: $\ge 65\%$ in 251st stock onwards. High alpha over $>7$ year horizons.
* **Flexi Cap Fund**: $\ge 65\%$ in equity with unrestricted cross-market cap allocation.
* **Multi Cap Fund**: Minimum 25% Large Cap, 25% Mid Cap, and 25% Small Cap mandatory at all times.
* **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)**: $\ge 80\%$ equity with mandatory 3-year lock-in (qualifies under Section 80C in Old Regime).
* **Balanced Advantage Fund (BAF) / Dynamic Asset Allocation**: Dynamically shifts equity from 0% to 100% based on valuation parameters (P/E, P/B, Momentum), maintaining equity taxation status ($\ge 65\%$ gross equity via arbitrage).
* **Liquid & Overnight Funds**: Debt maturities up to 91 days (Liquid) or 1 day (Overnight). Zero equity risk, primary vehicle for parking cash.

## 2.2 Mathematical Compounding & Portfolio Metrics

### Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Compounding Equation:
$$\text{Future Value} = P \times \left[ \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \right] \times (1 + i)$$
* $P$ = Monthly installment amount.
* $i = \frac{r}{12}$ (Monthly interest rate).
* $n = t \times 12$ (Total months).

### Step-Up SIP Escalation Formula:
Increasing SIP by $s\%$ annually (e.g., $10\%$ yearly step-up):
$$\text{SIP Installment in Year } k = P_1 \times (1 + s)^{k-1}$$
* Accelerates corpus accumulation by $60\% - 100\%$ compared to flat SIPs over a 15-year horizon.

### Risk-Adjusted Return Analytics:
* **Sharpe Ratio**: $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}$ (Excess return per unit of total standard deviation).
* **Sortino Ratio**: $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_d}$ (Excess return per unit of downside risk).
* **Beta ($\beta$)**: $\frac{\text{Cov}(R_p, R_m)}{\text{Var}(R_m)}$ (Market sensitivity).
* **Jensen's Alpha ($\alpha$)**: $R_p - \left[ R_f + \beta_p (R_m - R_f) \right]$ (True value-add over benchmark).

### Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) Tax Optimization:
* SWP redemptions are taxed **only on the capital gains component** of redeemed units, leaving principal return tax-exempt.
* Dramatically superior to Dividend (IDCW) payouts which are taxed at the investor's marginal slab rate (up to 30% + surcharge).

# SECTION 3: PERSONAL WEALTH ARCHITECTURE & RISK MANAGEMENT

## 3.1 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule Structure
* **50% Needs**: Mandatory living costs (Home loan EMI/Rent, utilities, groceries, health/term insurance, essential school fees).
* **30% Wants**: Discretionary lifestyle spending (Dining out, vacations, entertainment, luxury subscriptions).
* **20% Savings & Debt Elimination**: Mutual fund SIPs, PPF/EPF, emergency fund buildup, accelerated home loan principal prepayment.

---

## 3.2 Emergency Fund 3-Tier Liquidity Structure
* **Target Sizing**: **3 to 6 months of mandatory fixed monthly expenses** (6 to 12 months for freelancers, business owners, single-earner families).
* **Allocation**:
  1. **Tier 1 (20%)**: High-yield savings bank account (Instant ATM/UPI access).
  2. **Tier 2 (40%)**: Liquid Mutual Funds / Sweep-in Fixed Deposits (Same-day/T+1 access).
  3. **Tier 3 (40%)**: Ultra-Short Duration Funds / Short-Term Fixed Deposits.
* **Strict Rule**: Never place emergency reserves in equity funds, high-yield credit funds, real estate, or lock-in schemes (PPF/ELSS).

---

## 3.3 Strategic Asset Allocation & Rebalancing
* **Goal-Horizon Allocation**:
  * **Short-Term ($< 3\text{ Years}$)**: $0\%$ Equity / $100\%$ Debt & Liquid Funds.
  * **Medium-Term ($3\text{ to }7\text{ Years}$)**: $30\% - 50\%$ Equity (Aggressive Hybrid / BAF / Large Cap) / $50\% - 70\%$ Debt.
  * **Long-Term ($> 7\text{ Years}$)**: $65\% - 80\%$ Equity (Flexi Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap, Nifty 50) / $15\% - 25\%$ Debt / $5\% - 10\%$ Gold (SGBs / Gold ETFs).
* **Rebalancing Rule**: Rebalance portfolio annually or whenever equity allocation drifts by $\ge \pm 5\%$, locking in gains from outperforming assets and buying undervalued asset classes.

---

## 3.4 Insurance & Risk Mitigation
* **Term Life Insurance**: Pure term cover sized at **15 to 20 times annual gross income** plus liabilities minus liquid investments.
* **Health Insurance**: Base Family Floater (₹10–15 Lakhs) + Super Top-Up policy (₹50 Lakhs–₹1 Crore with deductible equal to base sum insured) for maximum cost-effective hospitalization protection.
